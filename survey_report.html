🌐 Foundation 3D Models

Models pre-trained on large-scale 3D datasets that serve as universal backbones for downstream segmentation tasks. Includes SAM3D (Meta), PonderV2, VISTA3D, MedDINOv3, CT-FM, and Triad. These models aim to generalize across anatomy, imaging modality, and downstream task.

🏥 Medical 3D Segmentation

Specialized and promptable models for volumetric medical images (CT, MRI). SAM-Med3D achieves zero-shot anatomical segmentation with just 1-2 prompt points. VISTA3D leads with 127-class SOTA. CNN-based models still dominate (BrainFM4Challenges).

☁️ Point Cloud & Specialized

Beyond medical: Mask3D (Transformer instance segmentation), Point-SAM (promptable), SAM2Point (zero-shot via video), SAM3D (2D→3D projection), GPT4Point (MLLM reasoning). Used in robotics, LiDAR, autonomous driving.

💡 Explainable AI for 3D

Making black-box 3D models interpretable: Agg²Exp (voxel attributions), Grad-CAM variants, FBI (point clouds), Causal XAI, XAI-CLIP, TorchEsegeta. Critical for clinical trust and debugging.

🔬 Microscopy & VEM Segmentation (micro-sam)

This section covers work related to micro-sam — the Segment Anything adaptation for microscopy. The open-source micro-sam provides interactive and automatic segmentation for biomedical microscopy (including volumetric EM/VEM) with outstanding zero-shot and fine-tuned performance.

📦 micro-sam — Segment Anything for Microscopy

The micro-sam project provides a general-purpose interactive and automatic segmentation tool tailored for microscopy images including volumetric electron microscopy (VEM). Key features: (1) Interactive segmentation using bounding box, point, and text prompts; (em>Automatic segmentation via instance segmentation; (3) Domain adaptation — support for light, electron, and cryo-EM; (4) 3D volume support.

Reference: Nature Methods (2024) ↗ — "Segment Anything for Microscopy" (Speiser, Bischof, Roy et al.).

💡 Explainable AI for 3D Segmentation

As 3D segmentation models enter clinical pipelines, explanation becomes critical: (1) Safety — detecting spurious correlations that harm patients; (2) Trust — clinicians need transparent decision rationale; (3) Debugging — diagnosing missegmentation; (4) Discovery — revealing novel biological insights from model attention.

Jul 2024

Agg²Exp ↗

Aggregated Attributions for 3D Segmentation. Addresses high-dimensional saliency maps (voxel × classes). Two-level aggregation: (1) voxels within segment → importance, (2) across classes. Gradient-based > perturbation-based on voxel attributions.

XAI
Voxel attribution SwinUNETR TotalSeg
Feb 2026

Causal XAI (PdCR) ↗

Explains segmentation via causal inference framework. ATE (average treatment effect) backpropagated into quantification metric. Reveals heterogeneity in perceptual strategies across foundational models.

XAI
Causal inference ATE Perception
Mar 2024

FBI (Feature Based Interpretability)

Fast pre-bottleneck feature norm XAI for point clouds. 3 orders of magnitude speedup. SOTA classification explainability. Analyzes rotation invariance, OOD robustness, dataset bias in 3D learning.

XAI
Point cloud Feature norm Fast XAI
Feb 2026

XAI-CLIP

ROI-Guided Perturbation using vision-language embeddings. 60% runtime reduction, +44.6% dice, +96.7% IoU for occlusion explanations. Clearer boundary-aware saliency on FLARE22/CHAOS.

XAI
CLIP ROI perturbation Multimodal
Oct 2021

TorchEsegeta

Unified framework for explainability of image-based deep learning. Extends classifiers to segmentation. Adapted for 3D vessel segmentation (brain MRA). Infidelity + sensitivity metrics for evaluation.

Unified framework 3D vessels

Human-machine-VLM system for computational pathology. Sliding-window experiments test explanation claims. VLM quantifies explanation predictiveness. Clinical path from explainable → explained AI.
